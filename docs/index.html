Prop cheat sheet · Week 4 · Mon Oct 5 – Thu Oct 8

Week 4 top 50 props

Every Kalshi yardage and receptions ladder across 2 games, scored for how likely it is to hit and whether the price still pays. One best line per player and stat, two props max per player.

How the score works
  • Sleeper projection (28%). Sleeper's Week 4 projection turned into a chance of clearing the line.
  • Player hit rate (20%). His games over this line this season, plus last season's games at a quarter weight each (all together worth at most 3 games), so one fluky game can't swing it.
  • Usage model (shares the projection weight). His recent share of the team's targets or carries times the team's volume per game, times his catch rate and yards per touch (shrunk toward a normal rate so one long play doesn't inflate it). Recent weeks count more. Blended 50/50 with the Sleeper projection.
  • Defense vs line (20%). How many of the opponent's 3 games the same role went over this exact line. Roles are by team depth: QB, lead RB, WR1, WR2, TE1, so a WR2 is compared to WR2s.
  • Price value (17%). The model's chance against the Kalshi price.
  • Defense rank (10%). Where the defense ranks among 32 teams in pass or rush yards allowed per game.
  • Agreement (5%). A small bonus when the projection, his history and the defense all say yes.
  • Safety penalties. Each game he missed the line costs 8%, each defense miss 5%, and thin markets (wide bid/ask) 10%.
  • Game script (up to ±8%). From the point spread and over/under. A big favorite leans on the run with a lead, so its lead back gets a boost and its passing a small cut; a big underdog throws to catch up, so its QB, receivers and pass-catching backs get a boost and its rushing a cut. A high or low team total nudges every yardage prop. Starts at a 3-point spread, full strength at 10.
  • QB situation (receivers, up to ±10%). If the QB who usually starts is out or not projected to start, the receiver's games with the new QB decide it (this season, and last season if they were teammates). No games together is a clear penalty; good history with the backup cancels it.
  • Game context (up to ±4%). A small nudge from things the stats can't see: wind and rain at kickoff, injuries on his team and the defense, his own injury status, recent ESPN headlines, and hype (most-added on Sleeper, Reddit buzz). Players listed out are dropped. It's kept small on purpose while it's tested. This run: lines 0 games; weather 0 outdoor games; injuries 173 players listed; news 2 players in headlines; sleeper trending 40 board players trending; reddit unavailable.
  • Kalshi %. The Kalshi yes price is the market's chance it hits; the number under it is the same price as sportsbook odds, before Kalshi's fee.
  • It learns. After each week the picks are graded and these weights and the model's chances are adjusted. See the Results tab.
  • Only lines priced 35¢ to 88¢ are eligible; anything pricier pays too little. Touchdown props are left out because they're too volatile to call safe.

+500$10 pays about $60

Real Kalshi prices for 0 of 60 parlays, as of Sun 1:49 PM. Expected prices use Kalshi's markup learned from 587 real quotes (about 2.8% per leg).

Where the odds come from. On each update the board builds the best parlays at +150 through +2000 and asks Kalshi for a real quote on each. Kalshi creates the combo, market makers quote it, and the request is cancelled without buying anything. Parlays with a quote show Kalshi quote. Any other parlay shows Expected Kalshi price: the legs' fair prices multiplied, plus the markup Kalshi has actually been adding to combos, learned from every real quote so far. If a leg has no seller showing, its price is estimated from the lines above and below it on the same ladder.

How legs are picked. Only board props, using whichever line on each ladder gets you to your odds with the best chance. Each leg's chance is a 50/50 blend of the model and Kalshi's price, with a 3% safety discount per leg. Rules: one leg per player, at most two per game (unless you pick one game), 2 to 6 legs. The three options share at most one player, and no player is in all three.

Quotes are as of the last update (Mon Oct 5, 6:55 PM PT) and move with the market, so confirm the price in Kalshi before you place it.

No graded weeks yet

The board is saved every update and each game's picks lock at its kickoff, so what gets graded is the board as it stood right before the game started (all of them are listed in data/locked_picks.csv in the repo). Once the games are played, the next update grades every pick against the box scores, shows the record here, and starts adjusting the model from what hit and what missed.
