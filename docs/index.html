Prop cheat sheet · Week 4 · Sun Oct 4 – Mon Oct 5

Week 4 top 50 props

Every Kalshi yardage and receptions ladder across 15 games, scored for how likely it is to hit and whether the price still pays. One best line per player and stat, two props max per player.

How the score works
  • Sleeper projection (28%). Sleeper's Week 4 projection turned into a chance of clearing the line.
  • Player hit rate (20%). His games over this line this season.
  • Defense vs line (20%). How many of the opponent's 3 games the same role went over this exact line. Roles are by team depth: QB, lead RB, WR1, WR2, TE1, so a WR2 is compared to WR2s.
  • Price value (17%). The model's chance against the Kalshi price.
  • Defense rank (10%). Where the defense ranks among 32 teams in pass or rush yards allowed per game.
  • Agreement (5%). A small bonus when the projection, his history and the defense all say yes.
  • Safety penalties. Each game he missed the line costs 8%, each defense miss 5%, and thin markets (wide bid/ask) 10%.
  • Game context (up to ±4%). A small nudge from things the stats can't see: the point spread and over/under, wind and rain at kickoff, injuries on his team and the defense, his own injury status, recent ESPN headlines, and hype (most-added on Sleeper, Reddit buzz). Players listed out are dropped. It's kept small on purpose while it's tested. This run: lines 15 games; weather 10 outdoor games; injuries 147 players listed; news 9 players in headlines; sleeper trending 41 board players trending; reddit unavailable.
  • Kalshi %. The Kalshi yes price is the market's chance it hits; the number under it is the same price as sportsbook odds, before Kalshi's fee.
  • It learns. After each week the picks are graded and these weights and the model's chances are adjusted. See the Results tab.
  • Only lines priced 35¢ to 88¢ are eligible; anything pricier pays too little. Touchdown props are left out because they're too volatile to call safe.
